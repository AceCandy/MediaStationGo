# Design
A MediaView repository query maps canonical season IDs to exact next-episode bindings for the three catalogs and applies library visibility. MediaProbeService exposes a scoped detail wake consumed by its existing coordinator. Detail scopes bypass only ingestion-specific episode exclusions and retain the missing-document predicate. They use the same executor with a cancelable one-second gap and cleanup disabled. Web GET detail and Emby Item invoke the same wake after visibility checks. Emby PlaybackInfoWithOptions invokes it after successful selection validation, covering both GET and POST without requiring detail access. No schema or protocol changes are required.
